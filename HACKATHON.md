## About

Connect your coding agent to AWS, build a real application, and ship it live for a chance to win $5,000 in AWS credits and an AWS Builder swag bundle.

Open to anyone with an AWS account, new or existing. Pick one of five app categories and a lane, Community or Startup. Shipping live is the whole point.

**Who can enter:** Builders 18 or older with a Builder Center profile. See Rules for country and employee exclusions.

## How to qualify

Every submission must include:

- A coding agent connected to the AWS console, with documented proof of the connection
- A live application running on AWS, reachable by a public URL
- One of five app categories (below)
- A lane: Community or Startup
- An original application that has not been published before
- A project post showing your development process, how the coding agent helped you ship, your category and lane, and a link to the live app

## The ship gate

Shipping is pass-or-fail. The project has to be live on AWS and reachable by the judges and the AI scoring system. A project that is not live does not advance to judging, no matter how strong the idea is.

## How submissions are judged

Two rounds after the ship gate:

1. **Round 1, AI scoring** — every qualifying submission is scored on creativity and storytelling, technical innovation, community and market impact, and communication quality. Top submissions advance.
2. **Round 2, human judging** — a panel of AWS experts reviews the shortlist and selects five winners across the categories.

## App categories (pick one)

| Category | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Workplace efficiency | Apps that help people and teams get more done: task automation, team dashboards, workflow engines |
| Daily life enhancement | Apps that make everyday life easier: smart home controls, personal assistants, habit trackers |
| Commercial potential | Apps built with a business in mind: SaaS tools, marketplace apps, vertical solutions |
| Personal expression | Apps that help people create and share: art generators, music tools, content platforms |
| Social good | Measurable improvements for underserved populations across Education, Health, and Climate resilience |

## Lane (pick one)

The lane is about intent and audience, not technology. Any category project can run in either lane.

- **Startup** — a project pushed toward becoming a product, with a story about where it is headed. Best for builders thinking about product-market fit, a first user base, or a path to a real business.
- **Community** — a project that helps a group the builder belongs to (a class, a local meetup, an open-source project). Best for builders solving a problem for people around them.

### Social Good and AWS Social Impact Credits

Builder Center and Zero to Shipped are partnering with the AWS Skilling and Social Impact team on the Social Good category criteria so participating organizations can be eligible for AWS Social Impact Credits. Projects delivering measurable impact and meeting the eligibility requirements may qualify to apply for these credits.

## Tagging your submission

Tags route your project to the right category and lane for scoring. Every submission must carry **two** tags, added on your Builder Center project before the deadline.

- **App category tag (pick one):** `#workplace-efficiency`, `#daily-life-enhancement`, `#commercial-potential`, `#social-good`, `#personal-expression`
- **Lane tag (pick one):** `#startup`, `#community`
