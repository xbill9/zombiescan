## Submission requirements

To submit an eligible entry, participants must:

1. Connect a coding agent to the AWS console before or during the Submission Period;
2. Build and ship a live application on AWS that is publicly reachable by judges and the AI scoring system;
3. Choose one of five app categories: Workplace Efficiency, Daily Life Enhancement, Commercial Potential, Social Good, or Personal Expression;
4. Choose a focus track: Community or Startup;
5. Publish a project on AWS Builder Center that includes: (a) proof of coding agent connection, (b) description of the project and development process, (c) designation of app category and focus track, and (d) a link or URL to the live application;
6. Create an original application that has not been previously published; and
7. Document their use of AWS services and coding agent in their submission.

## Ship gate (pass/fail)

Every Submission is subject to a mandatory ship gate verification. To qualify for judging, the project must:

- Be live on AWS and reachable via a public URL at the time of evaluation
- Include documented proof of coding agent connection to the AWS console
- Be accessible to both the AI scoring system and human judges

A project that is not live and reachable does not advance to judging, regardless of the quality of the idea. Pass/fail, no exceptions.

## Evaluation process

After the Submission Period closes on October 2, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. PT, AWS evaluates all qualifying Submissions (those that pass the ship gate) through two gates.

**Gate 1 — AI scoring and human review (week of October 5, 2026).** All qualifying Submissions are scored by a combination of AI and human review on:

- Technical Innovation & Originality (25%)
- Implementation Quality (25%)
- Community/Market Impact (25%)
- Creativity & Storytelling (25%)

The top 100 Submissions by composite score advance to Gate 2.

**Gate 2 — Human judging (week of October 12, 2026).** A panel of AWS experts and community leaders evaluates the top 100 Submissions on the same four criteria (25% each) and selects 5 winners across the app categories.
